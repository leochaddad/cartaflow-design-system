# Voice and content

## Who we talk to

| Audience | They want | We sound |
|---|---|---|
| Pessoas físicas | Send one letter without a printer or a queue | Warm, simple, reassuring |
| Empresas | Automate notices, boletos, bulk mail | Efficient, concrete, numbers-first |
| Desenvolvedores | An API that just works | Precise, technical, example-first |
| Foreign companies (EN) | Reach Brazilian addresses without a local operation | Clear, credible, local-expert |

## Portuguese (primary)

- Brazilian Portuguese as people actually speak it. "Você", never "o senhor" or "tu".
- Short sentences. One idea each. Active voice.
- Concrete nouns: "carta", "envelope", "endereço", "CEP". Not "soluções", "ecossistema", "sinergia".
- Buttons start with a verb in the infinitive: "Enviar carta", "Criar conta", "Ver preços", "Ler a documentação". Max 3 words.
- Headings say the benefit or the fact, not a slogan. "Envie cartas pela internet" beats "O futuro da correspondência".
- SEO keywords appear naturally once in the title, once in the H1 or intro, and where they genuinely fit. Never stuffed.

## English (international)

- Written for a foreign company deciding whether CartaFlow can handle their Brazilian mail. Not a translation of the Portuguese page.
- Explain the Brazilian context they do not know: CEP is the postal code, CPF/CNPJ are tax IDs, Correios is the national postal service, boletos are payment slips.
- American English spelling, plain business tone.

## Mechanics (both languages)

- **No em dashes (—)** and no en dashes as separators. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses. Ranges use a hyphen: 34-266.
- No exclamation marks in interface text.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons ("Envio de cartas em massa", not "Envio De Cartas Em Massa").
- Numbers: Brazilian format in Portuguese (R$ 1.234,56; 1.000 cartas), US format in English (BRL 1,234.56).
- Dates: 13 de outubro de 2026 / October 13, 2026. Times in 24 h in Portuguese.
- Use real, specific examples, never round fake statistics ("99,9%", "10.000 clientes") and never invented testimonials.

## Claims we can and cannot make

Allowed (confirmed by the business model):
- We print, envelope and post physical letters in Brazil.
- Customers send content digitally (written online or PDF) via web or API.
- Pricing: monthly subscription plus a per-letter charge; plans Free, Pro and Scale; the thresholds where each plan becomes cheaper.
- OTP address verification by letter exists as a product.

Not allowed until operations confirms in writing:
- Delivery times or SLAs ("entrega em 2 dias").
- Tracking ("rastreie sua carta") or specific postal modalities (registrada, AR, Sedex).
- International prices or any guarantee of delivery.
- Volumes, customer counts or logos of customers.

When a visitor will ask about one of these, answer honestly: "Os prazos dependem da modalidade postal e do destino. Informamos a estimativa antes do envio." Keep that sentence pattern consistent across pages.

## Microcopy patterns

| Situation | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Empty state | What is missing + one action | "Nenhuma carta enviada ainda. Enviar a primeira carta" |
| Error | What happened + how to fix | "Não encontramos este CEP. Confira os 8 dígitos." |
| Success | Past tense, specific | "Carta enviada para impressão." |
| Destructive confirm | Name the object and the consequence | "Excluir o rascunho 'Cobrança março'? Isso não pode ser desfeito." |
| Loading | Say what is happening | "Gerando a prévia da carta" |
